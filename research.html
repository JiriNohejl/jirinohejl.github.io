01 / Dissertation

George Mason University
PhD expected May 2027

Computational Monetary Systems:
Emergence, Networks, and Design

How does the architecture of a monetary system shape its operation?

My dissertation examines digital currency networks through agent-based modeling and empirical data. A central project, Digital Currency Architecture: Networks and Design, studies the interaction of blockchain fees, heterogeneous users, and liquidity across multiple network layers.

I investigate the emergence of network hubs, payment bottlenecks, and costly channel rebalancing, and explore alternative designs. The empirical work integrates blockchain, mempool, and second-layer network data.

Approach · Monetary theory, agent-based modeling, network analysis, and empirical data.

02 / AI & markets

Research prototype
Public code and analysis

AI Decision-Making in Experimental Markets

How much of a market’s performance comes from its rules—and how much from the decision-making of its participants?

I compare AI decision agents with a standard zero-intelligence trading baseline in a continuous double auction. The project examines allocative efficiency and price discovery while holding the trading institution fixed.

The public benchmark provides an initial comparison within a controlled simulation. Its model, recorded results, and analysis notebook make the experiment available for inspection.

03 / Research direction

Proposed extension

Digital Money for AI Agents

What monetary and payment arrangements help agents transact on behalf of people?

I am interested in connecting my work on digital monetary networks with experiments in AI decision-making. A proposed extension would compare how payment access, liquidity requirements, and delegated spending rules affect trade and market outcomes.

Questions for future work

Payments, delegation, and competition

How do different payment arrangements change the cost of exchange? What happens when agents face explicit budgets? How do those choices affect users’ gains from trade and the concentration of market activity?
