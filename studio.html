Model & data notes

How the model works

Output per worker is y = A k^α, where A is total factor productivity, k is capital per worker, and α is the capital share. Each year, capital changes according to k(next) = [(1 − δ)k + sy] / (1 + n), and productivity grows according to A(next) = A(1 + gA). Each regime’s parameters govern the transition from that year to the next.

With fixed parameters, output per worker grows at (1 + gA)^(1 / (1 − α)) − 1 on the balanced growth path. The displayed paths depend on the assumptions you choose. TFP fitting adjusts productivity growth by regime to reduce the root mean square error in log output over the selected sample.

Upload format & teaching samples

Upload a CSV with year,y,s,n,delta,k. Include at least five consecutive years, with one row per year. Enter positive output per worker (y) and capital per worker (k) in thousands of constant dollars on the same price basis. Enter the investment rate (s), labor force growth (n), and depreciation (delta) as fractions such as 0.25 for 25%.

The bundled country series are illustrative teaching samples with unverified sources. Uploaded data stays in your browser and starts with one regime. Resetting or choosing a dataset clears your saved comparison and model changes.
