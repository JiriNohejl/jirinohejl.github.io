01 / Digital money

Dissertation research

Digital Currency Architecture

A computational approach to networks, liquidity, and monetary design.

I built a relational data pipeline integrating blockchain, mempool, and second-layer network data, alongside an agent-based model informed by empirical patterns from Bitcoin and the Lightning Network.

The model examines how heterogeneous users, fee dynamics, and liquidity allocation interact across a monetary network. It supports investigation of hubs, bottlenecks, and channel rebalancing.

02 / AI & market design

Research prototype
Public repository

Continuous Double Auction:
AI Market Benchmark

How do different traders perform under the same auction rules?

This experiment compares constrained zero-intelligence traders, following Gode and Sunder (1993), with agents using TypeSafe AI’s Jev decision model. Both trade through a continuous double auction with an active order book.

The repository includes the model, a paired simulation benchmark, recorded results, an analysis notebook, and a locally runnable market interface.

Methods · Agent-based market simulation, paired comparisons, and statistical analysis. Reported values are from the public repository’s benchmark table.

Growth and Technological Change

Learn growth theory by experimenting with it.

A visual laboratory for undergraduate macroeconomics: predict what will happen, change an assumption, and explain the result. Explore convergence, transitional dynamics, and the distinction between higher output and faster long-run growth.

An experiment in growth

What drives the growth path?

Solow model

Change an assumption.
Compare the resulting paths.

Keep total factor productivity (TFP) growth fixed and raise investment. Compare the effect on the level of output with the effect on its long-run growth rate.

Experiment assumptions

The baseline uses an investment rate of 25%, annual TFP growth of 1%, annual labor force growth of 1%, annual depreciation of 5%, and a capital share of one third. Both paths start with output per worker indexed to 100.
