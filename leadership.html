01 / Economic policy

Chief Economist
2018–present

Institut for Liberal Studies Prague (INLiST)

Applied economics, public communication, and education.

As chief economist, I contribute economic analysis and commentary on institutions, governance, and monetary policy. My public work includes policy submissions, essays, radio debates, podcasts, and educational programmes.

The role connects academic ideas with concrete policy questions. Examples include a joint submission on Prague taxi regulation, writing on the measurement of the tax burden, and commentary on digital taxation and pension reform.

  • Policy analysis: translating economic reasoning into writing and public discussion.
  • Public communication: explaining monetary and institutional questions across written and recorded formats.
  • Education: lectures and seminars on monetary systems, institutions, and economic transformation.

02 / Institution building

Co-Founder · Ski Education Manager
Engagement since 2018

Lithuanian Snowsports Instructors Association (LSIA)

Developing professional education and the organization that supports it.

I co-founded LSIA and helped develop its governance, internal operations, and digital strategy. My work has included instructor education, assessment processes, teaching concepts, and international collaboration.

I joined as Technical Director in 2018, with responsibilities spanning education-system design, digital strategy, and international professional development. I am currently listed by the association as Ski Education Manager and an Alpine Trainer / Examiner.

  • Organizational development: helping establish governance, operations, and a digital strategy.
  • Education and assessment: developing professional learning and evaluation processes.
  • International collaboration: connecting expertise across instructor education organizations.
